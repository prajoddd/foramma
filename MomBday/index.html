Dear Amma,

Happiest Birthday ❤️

I don't think I'll ever be able to put into words everything you've done for me.

You've worked so hard, sacrificed so much, and always found a way to make our lives easier and happier.

There are so many little things you do every day that might seem ordinary, but to me they mean everything.

Every time you put us before yourself, every time you worry about us, every time you make sure we're okay — I see it.

I may not always say thank you. I may not always show it. But please know that I notice and appreciate all of it.

You have given me so much more than I could ever ask for. You've given me love, strength, comfort, and a place that always feels like home.

On your birthday, I just want you to remember something: you deserve all the happiness you give to everyone else.

I hope this year brings you more reasons to smile, more moments to rest, and more happiness than you can imagine.

And if I could give you one thing in return for everything you've given me...

It would be all the happiness in the world.

Thank you for being my mom. Thank you for everything.

I love you more than words can ever explain.

Happiest Birthday, Amma. ❤️

With all my love, Prajod Krishna ♥
